Why ACC FOV is Different

Assetto Corsa Competizione does NOT use vertical FOV like the original Assetto Corsa. ACC uses horizontal FOV (hFOV), measured across the width of your monitor at the center. If you copy a 30° vFOV value from AC into ACC, you'll get a tunnel-vision 50° hFOV and wonder why you can't see apexes.

This acc fov calculator solves it: we compute the exact hFOV from your screen width and eye distance. Most drivers run ACC at the default 70° hFOV, which is often 15-30° too narrow on ultrawides and 20° too wide on singles. The result? Distances compress, curbs look closer, and you brake early at Monza T1. Using a proper assetto corsa competizione fov value restores 1:1 scale, and your consistency improves within two sessions.

Bottom line for ACC: correct hFOV = real-world scale. It's not a preference slider — it's optics. Use this acc fov calculator once, lock it, and adjust seat position instead.

ACC Triple Screen Setup — Full Guide

ACC has excellent triple support because Unreal Engine renders three independent view frustums. This sim racing triple screen fov calculator is built for it.

Step 1 — Enable triples in ACC: Options > Video > check "Triple Screen." Restart the game.

FAQ — Essential Questions

1. Why is ACC FOV different from Assetto Corsa?

Assetto Corsa Competizione uses horizontal FOV (hFOV), unlike the original Assetto Corsa which uses vertical FOV (vFOV). Using a vFOV number in ACC will result in a severely zoomed-in view.

2. How do I set up a Samsung Odyssey G9 for ACC?

ACC treats the G9 as a single ultrawide screen. Set the calculator to Single monitor, 32:9 aspect ratio, and 1000R curve. The resulting hFOV (typically around 101-104 degrees) should be entered in the ACC View Settings.

3. Does curve radius matter in ACC?

Yes. A 1000R curve on a wide monitor brings the edges closer to your eyes, slightly increasing your effective FOV. Our calculator dynamically adjusts for this curve.

4. Should I change FOV to see the mirrors better?

No. Increasing FOV distorts the track scale. Instead, adjust the in-game seat position backwards or adjust the virtual mirror settings.
